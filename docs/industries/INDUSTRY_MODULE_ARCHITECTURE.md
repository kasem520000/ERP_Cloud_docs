## 1. The universal business model

One shared accounting/transaction core; businesses differ by **configuration, not code**:

```
Industry template (terminology, navigation, workflow defaults, report pack, numbering)
  → Country pack (tax, compliance)
    → Plan entitlements (materialized per tenant)
      → Tenant settings → Branch settings
        → Role permissions (restrict-only)
          → Resolved workspace (cached, content-hashed, event-invalidated)
```

The configuration engine has **ten resolution layers** with deterministic precedence per
value class, fail-closed validation, and a golden effective-config hash per
(template × plan × jurisdiction) asserted in CI — configuration can never alter
accounting math (INV-022 config-fuzz suite).

## 2. Core vs optional vs industry (capability tiers)

| Tier | Examples | Ownership |
|------|----------|-----------|
| Core (all tenants) | CoA, journals, partners, items, cash/bank, AR/AP, tax engine, reporting, printing | Platform modules (PHASE-006…013) |
| Optional platform modules | POS, manufacturing, CRM/contracts/projects, distribution | Platform modules, entitlement-gated (PHASE-016, 018, 019, 017) |
| Industry templates | 8 first-party families below | Manifests applied at onboarding, additive-only |

New business needs are met by: (1) entitlements + settings, (2) workflow policies,
(3) a new capability in the owning module, (4) a new industry template — **never** by
touching the accounting core or by forking modules. Templates may **not** introduce new
accounting, isolation, or authorization primitives; such needs require an ADR
(industries rule R1).

Machine-readable manifests are extracted to [templates/manifests/](templates/manifests/)

feature keys, numbering formats, inventory defaults, navigation, and pinned report keys.
Template **switching is additive-only** with 7 governance tests (switch-safety, golden
onboarding fixtures, capability closure) — [README.md](../README.md) §5.

## 5. Billing/entitlements interaction

Materialized entitlements with sources/expiry; server-only enforcement; plan limits
**warn and bill but never hard-block a document posting** (POS-friendly); read-only grace
is a first-class tenant state; expiring admin overrides are audited; manual-invoicing
fallback for cardless markets. Prices/limits are PLACEHOLDER pending OQ-002 —
commercial model remains a product decision ([../product/SUBSCRIPTION_AND_ENTITLEMENTS.md](../product/SUBSCRIPTION_AND_ENTITLEMENTS.md)).
